`./run.sh` should take the workspace from its initial state and complete the task end-to-end. Whatever steps your implementation needs must live inside `./run.sh`. Your implementation should use only `josh` primitives (`.josh`, `.jshc`, and `.jshd`) and SHOULD NOT use python or bash in `./run.sh`, beyond what is necessary to invoke `josh`.
